Journal publishing is broken

Researchers spend 8+ months and pay up to $10,000 to get work published. Readers pay again to read it.

The money flows to publishers, not the people creating or benefiting from knowledge.

A reminder from 1613

Galileo’s letter to Benedetto Castelli showed empirical evidence that Earth is not the unmoving center of the universe.

Without this free disemmination of knowledge, modern astronomy would not have been possible.

We’re building the fix

Unseal keeps open, rapid sharing alive: publish with no fees, no wait times, and no gatekeeping—then hear back from readers instantly.
