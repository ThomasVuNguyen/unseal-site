

Unseal

Publish knowledge and receive feedbacks

No wait time, no fee, direct interaction with your readers.

If you share or consume scienfic knowledge in the internet, this is for you.

The current system of publishing knowledge to journals is broken.

You work hard to produce something great.
You work hard to put it together in the right format.
You fight for a chance to pay publishers up to $10,000.

Just to leave your readers to pay to read that.

All the labor and dollar was made to benefit these publishers, not the producer of knowledge or consumer/benefactor of such knowledge.

Alternatives do exist, but they don't contribute to your career growth as a researcher.

This is broken, and the publishers make a lot of money from this "oligopoly".

It wasn't always like this. Scientific knowledge used to be distributed out in the open.

If Gallileo had to pay 10k and wait 12 months to publish his findings, our current generation would still believe Earth is the center of the Universe (which it's not btw).

Hi, I'm Thomas and I want to solve this issue. 

I want to build a platform where people can share scientific knowledge and get feedback from others. Truly open, no gated paywall, no crazy evaluation wait time. 

To do that, I need your help - share with me your ideas on how this could be built, and how YOU want this platform built?

- What do you care about when it comes to peer review process?
- What do you care about when it comes to how others use your knowledge? to build new knowledge, to build applications, to save people etc.
- Anything you want to share

Comment below!

And sign up in the waitlist to test out the platform on release.

Together, let's build a world where scientific knowledge is properly evaluated, shared, and benefits our species as a whole.
